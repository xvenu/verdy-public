Compliance Policy

Sanctions and eligibility

Service is not available to sanctioned persons, entities, or regions. Payments that cannot clear compliance review are rejected before provisioning — no service, no charge ambiguity.

Financial records

Every charge maps to a numbered order and invoice retained per applicable law. Refunds and chargebacks are recorded with status, never silently dropped.

Lawful disclosure

We disclose account records only on valid legal process, notify affected customers unless gagged, and publish any material disclosures in the Trust Center. We do not retain proxied traffic content, so there is nothing to disclose about it.

Beta honesty

Where compliance tooling is still maturing (e.g. automated sanctions screening), manual review applies and is disclosed at checkout rather than hidden.
