Service Level Policy

Targets (not yet warranties)

During public beta these are targets we operate against, measured internally: verified-payment provisioning in seconds; node allow-list sync every 5 minutes; support first response under 24h business days; abuse triage 24h; security acknowledgement 72h. Uptime percentages are not yet measured and are not claimed — see Status.

Credits

Verified outages longer than 24h earn extension credit equal to the outage length, applied automatically where measurable, otherwise on ticket request. No credit lotteries: the rule is mechanical.

Exclusions

Target blocks, site-side bot defenses, customer misconfiguration, and force majeure are outside service levels — though support still helps debug them.
