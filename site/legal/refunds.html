Refund Policy

Eligibility

Unused time is refundable within 7 days of payment when the service was not usable for reasons on our side (verified outage, failed provisioning). Used bandwidth and consumed time are non-refundable. Abuse-related suspensions are never refunded.

Process

Open a support ticket with the invoice number. Approved refunds are attempted with the original provider (e.g. Paystack refund API), recorded with status, and the subscription is cancelled. Every step is audit-logged and visible in your transaction history.

Timelines

Provider refunds settle on provider timelines (typically 5–10 business days for cards; crypto refunds return to your sending address). If a provider refund fails, the subscription credit alternative is offered.

Chargebacks

File a dispute with us first — chargebacks freeze the account during investigation and slow everyone down. Fraudulent chargebacks are treated as abuse under the Acceptable Use Policy.
