Privacy Policy

What we do not collect

We do not log the content, destinations, or timing of your proxied traffic. Proxy nodes authenticate logins and forward bytes; they keep no traffic history.

What we collect and why

Account data (name, email, phone, country, credentials) — to provide the service. Billing data (orders, payments, invoices, refunds) — to charge correctly and comply with financial record-keeping. Support data (tickets, operator notes) — to resolve issues. Security data (login attempts, audit log, abuse reports) — to prevent fraud and abuse. See Cookies for browser storage.

Retention

Active account data for the account lifetime; billing records as required by applicable law; security logs up to 12 months; deleted accounts purged per our deletion workflow except records the law requires us to keep (see Compliance).

Sharing

Payment details go to the payment provider you choose (Paystack, Stripe, crypto gateway) and nowhere else. We do not sell personal data. We disclose only when legally compelled, and challenge overbroad requests where possible.

Your rights

Request access, correction, export, or deletion via a support ticket. Deletion stops service at the next node sync per the documented workflow.

Processors

Sub-processor terms are in the Data Processing Policy. Questions: see Contact.
