Privacy Policy — Verdy Proxy

Verdy Proxy ("Verdy", "we", "us") is a paid proxy service at verdy.vernerx.com: customers create an account, buy a proxy plan, and receive private HTTP/HTTPS + SOCKS5 proxy credentials in 11 countries. This policy explains exactly what personal data we collect when you use our website, dashboard, sign-in options, billing, and support — why we collect it, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it.

1. Data we collect

Account data. When you sign up directly we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional in some flows), selected country, and password. Passwords are never stored in readable form — only a salted cryptographic hash.

Google sign-in data. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google sends us the basic profile covered by the scopes we request (openid email profile): your name, email address, profile picture URL (when available), and your Google account ID. We use this solely to identify your account and prevent duplicates. We do not request Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google data, and we do not use your Google data for advertising.

GitHub sign-in data. If you choose "Continue with GitHub", GitHub sends us the data covered by the user:email scope: your GitHub username, account ID, and email address(es). We use this solely to identify your account. We do not request repository, organization, or code access.

Email verification codes. If you use email-code login, we store a one-way hash of the 6-digit code with a 5-minute expiry and a 5-attempt cap. Plain codes are emailed once and never stored.

Billing data. Orders, plan selections, payment method, payment references, amounts, currencies, invoices, coupon use, and refund records. Card and bank details are entered on and processed by our payment providers (Paystack, Stripe) — full card numbers never touch our servers. For crypto we record the transaction hash you submit plus provider webhook confirmations.

Service and subscription data. Your proxy username, subscription type and country, quota usage, activation and expiry timestamps, and node allow-list sync timestamps. This is the minimum needed to switch your credentials on and off correctly.

Support data. Support tickets you open (subject, messages, attached account context) and operator notes written while resolving them.

Security and fraud-prevention data. Login attempts (for rate limiting), session tokens, an append-only audit log of account and admin actions, and abuse reports. This data protects accounts and the network.

Technical log data. Standard web-server logs (IP address, user agent, pages requested, timestamps) used for security, debugging, and capacity planning.

2. What we do NOT collect

We do not log the content, destinations, or timing of your proxied traffic. Proxy nodes authenticate your login and forward bytes; they keep no traffic history. Analytics on the marketing site, if any, are limited to aggregate page views — no cross-site tracking profiles.

3. How we use your data

We use personal data only to: create and secure your account; authenticate you (password, email code, or Google/GitHub sign-in); provision, meter, renew, or suspend proxy access; process orders, payments, invoices, and refunds; provide support; detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and account takeover; and meet legal (e.g. tax record-keeping) obligations. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Cookies and browser storage

We use strictly-necessary session cookies to keep you logged in and remember security preferences. Details are on the Cookies page. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

5. Who we share data with

Payment providers — the provider you choose at checkout (Paystack, Stripe, or a crypto confirmation service) receives the payment details needed to charge you. Login providers — Google or GitHub, only during the sign-in handshake you initiate. Infrastructure providers — our hosting provider (server hosting), content-delivery/security network in front of this site, and email-delivery provider for transactional mail (codes, invoices). Each sees only the data needed for its function. We disclose data to authorities only when legally compelled, and we challenge overbroad requests where possible. Full processor terms are in the Data Processing Policy.

6. Where data lives and transfers

Account, billing, and support data is stored on our dashboard servers (currently Germany) with encrypted off-site-capable backups. Proxy authentication metadata necessarily travels to the proxy exit country you select so nodes can authorize you. By using the service you understand your data may be processed in these locations under the safeguards in this policy and our DPA.

7. How long we keep data

Active account and subscription data for the account lifetime. Email-code hashes expire within minutes by design. Session tokens expire when you log out or they time out. Billing records are kept as long as tax and financial law requires. Security and server logs up to 12 months. Database backups rotate on a 14-day cycle plus provider snapshots. Deleted accounts are purged through our deletion workflow except records the law requires us to retain (see Compliance).

8. How we protect data

TLS in transit throughout; salted password hashing (passwords never stored readably); one-way hashes for email codes; least-privilege server access; append-only audit logging of sensitive actions; daily database backups with restricted permissions. No system is perfectly secure — report weaknesses privately per our disclosure policy, never via public issue.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of your personal data, and may object to or restrict certain processing. Exercise these rights at any time by opening a support ticket from your dashboard (fastest, arrives with account context) or via the Contact page. We respond within 30 days. Deletion ends the service: credentials stop working at the next node sync and billing records required by law are retained in anonymized or legally-required form.

10. Children

Verdy Proxy is a paid service intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes materially we update the "Updated" date above and note it on the Blog or dashboard. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or rights requests: open a support ticket from your dashboard or use the Contact page. Security issues: follow the Responsible Disclosure process.
