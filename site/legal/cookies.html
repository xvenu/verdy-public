Cookie Policy

What we store

Session cookies (Sid for customers, Asid for admins): HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure+HSTS in production, 12-hour life. They hold a random token only — nothing readable. Affiliate referral: a first-party cookie remembering a referral code for the configured cookie window. Local preferences: notification toggles stored in your own browser via localStorage, never sent to us.

What we don't use

No third-party trackers, no advertising cookies, no cross-site analytics beacons on the dashboard or marketing pages.

Control

Clearing cookies signs you out (sessions are server-side and expire regardless). Blocking all cookies will prevent login — the portal requires its session cookie to function.
