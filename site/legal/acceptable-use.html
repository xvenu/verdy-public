Acceptable Use Policy

Prohibited uses

Spam and phishing; credential stuffing and account takeover; DDoS and network attacks; fraud, scams, and money laundering; CSAM and any sexual exploitation; malware distribution and botnets; sanctions evasion and any illegal activity. Scraping must respect target terms and the law — aggressive or deceptive collection patterns are also prohibited.

Enforcement

Valid abuse reports are triaged within 24h (Abuse Handling Policy). Consequences scale: warning, throttling, suspension, termination without refund, and referral to authorities for illegal activity.

Appeals

Suspended for a false positive? Open a ticket with evidence (timestamps, job descriptions, target domains). We review with data, not vibes — and we publish aggregate enforcement honesty in the Trust Center.
