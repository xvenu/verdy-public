Abuse Handling Policy

Reporting

Email abuse@TODO-beta-domain with timestamps (UTC), source IPs, destination, and logs or samples. Reports without actionable evidence are acknowledged but cannot proceed — tell us exactly what to look at.

Triage (24h)

Valid reports are matched against account and audit records: warning, throttling, suspension, or termination depending on severity, intent, and history. Illegal activity is referred to authorities.

Proportionality

Shared infrastructure means one bad actor can burn everyone's IPs. We act fast against clear abuse and publish aggregate enforcement notes in the Trust Center — without naming reporters or exposing customer data.

Appeals

Suspension notices include the reason class and an appeal path via support ticket. False positives are reversed with expiry restored.
