Data Processing Policy

Roles

For account, billing, and support data you provide, Verdy acts as controller; for data you route through proxies, we are a conduit — we do not inspect, store, or mine proxied traffic content.

Sub-processors

Payment providers you choose (Paystack, Stripe, crypto gateway), email delivery for OTP codes, and our hosting providers. Each processes only what its function requires. We will update this list before engaging new sub-processors.

Security measures

Secret files at 0600 with no repository access, role-gated admin tooling, audit logging of sensitive actions, signed payment webhooks, rate-limited authentication, encrypted backups with tested restores, and TLS on all public endpoints.

Deletion and return

On verified deletion request: credentials revoked at the next node sync, sessions terminated, operational data purged; billing records retained only as the law requires. No data export lock-in — invoices and transaction history export as CSV/PDF anytime.
