Billing guide

Orders are the source of truth

Checkout creates a numbered order (product, cycle, country, type, coupon, USD amount). Payments only satisfy orders — nothing activates on payment alone.

Verification before provisioning

Every provider callback must pass signature, replay, amount, and order checks. Only then is a subscription created, resources allocated, an invoice numbered, and proxy access extended. Failed verifications mark the payment failed with an audit entry — never silent.

Cycles and coupons

Monthly ×1, quarterly ×3 minus 10%, yearly ×12 minus 20%. Coupon codes apply percentage discounts with expiry and usage caps enforced at order time.

Invoices and history

Every verified charge gets an INV-YYYY-NNNN invoice, printable with PDF download. Transactions filter by date, status, and method with CSV export.

Renewals and refunds

Renewals stack from the later of now or expiry. Refunds are requested through support, attempted with the provider, and recorded with status — approved refunds cancel the subscription.
