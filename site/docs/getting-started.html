Getting started in 5 steps

1. Create your account

Sign up with email + password, an email confirmation code, or Google/GitHub. Pick a proxy type and country — both are changeable later.

2. Choose a product

Billing works on products (e.g. Residential 20GB, Static 5), not raw countries. Monthly, quarterly (−10%), and yearly (−20%) cycles are priced from the USD catalog on the Pricing page.

3. Pay

Checkout creates a numbered order; your payment satisfies it. Paystack, Stripe, USDT, USDC verify automatically. The order page shows live status.

4. Get credentials

On verification the delivery screen shows host, username, password, expiry, plus TXT/CSV/JSON downloads. Nothing to search for.

5. Connect

curl -x http://USER:PASS@usa.proxy.verdy.vernerx.com:8899 https://api.ipify.org

If it prints an IP, you're live. If you get 407, see Troubleshooting.
