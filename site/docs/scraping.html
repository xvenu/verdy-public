Scraping patterns that survive

Rotate identity, not just IP

Rotate user-agents, session ids, and request timing together. A fresh IP with a fingerprintable bot signature still gets blocked — and burns quota.

Respect the target

Obey robots.txt and terms, cache aggressively, crawl off-peak, and keep concurrency modest. All use must comply with our Acceptable Use Policy — abuse reports risk suspension.

Backoff is a feature

429/503 → exponential backoff with jitter. Never retry-storm: our edge rate-limits per key, and the target will too.

Match product to job

Bulk discovery on Datacenter or Rotating Residential; login-walled flows on Static or Mobile; price checks on sticky sessions so the cart and the listing share an identity.
