FAQ

What happens when quota runs out?

Quota is tracked against your subscription and shown in Usage; node-level hard cutoffs ship with traffic telemetry. Until then, renew or upgrade on schedule — support can confirm your remaining balance anytime.

Rotating or sticky for logins?

Neither — logins belong on Static. Residential rotation will log you out.
