How charging works

Checkout creates a numbered order first. Money only satisfies the order; service activates only after the provider webhook verifies signature, amount, and reference. You get a numbered invoice for every charge.

Coupons

Percentage coupons apply at order time (e.g. launch codes). Validity windows and usage caps are enforced, not promised.

Renewals never steal time

Extensions stack from the later of now or your current expiry. Renewing early costs nothing extra.

Refunds

Real recorded process with provider refund attempts — see the Refund Policy. No "contact us and hope" black hole.
