How Verdy provisioning actually works

2026-09-17 · Verdy Team · 3 min

Most proxy dashboards activate you by hand. Ours can't — there is no code path where a payment directly enables service. Here is what happens instead.

1. The order comes first

Checkout creates a numbered order: product, cycle, country, type, coupon, USD amount. The order is the source of truth; money only ever satisfies an order.

2. The provider talks to a webhook

Paystack, Stripe, Daraja, or NOWPayments calls back. The handler checks four things in order: signature, replay (seen this event before?), amount (within tolerance of the order), and order existence. Any failure marks the payment failed with an audit entry.

3. Provisioning runs once

On success the engine creates a subscription, records your resource allocation (bandwidth quota or proxy count), numbers an invoice, and extends your proxy expiry. Run the same webhook twice and the second call finds the existing subscription and stops — idempotency is structural, not a flag somebody remembers to check.

Why this matters to you

Your invoice number, order id, and subscription line up 1:1:1. When support investigates, there is exactly one chain of records to follow — which is why tickets get answered with data.
