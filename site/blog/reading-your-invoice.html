Reading your Verdy invoice

2026-09-17 · Verdy Team · 2 min

Every Verdy charge produces an INV-YYYY-NNNN invoice. Here is what each line means and how to verify it yourself.

Order id

The ord_… reference ties the invoice to the checkout you approved — product, cycle, country, type, and coupon. Support can pull the full order from this id alone.

Amount and method

USD amount plus the method used (Paystack Card, M-Pesa, USDT…). M-Pesa invoices also carry the KES figure converted at order time — USD remains the source of truth.

Reference

The provider's transaction reference (Paystack reference, M-Pesa receipt, crypto payment id). Match it against your bank, M-Pesa SMS, or block explorer to confirm the charge independently.

Status and PDF

issued means verified and provisioned. Download the PDF for your books; transactions export to CSV. Anything unclear becomes a support ticket with the invoice number — which is all we need to investigate.
