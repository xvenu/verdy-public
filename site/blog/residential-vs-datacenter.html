Residential vs datacenter: an honest guide

2026-09-17 · Verdy Team · 3 min

Every proxy seller wants you on residential — it costs more. Here is when to ignore that advice.

Buy datacenter when…

Targets don't fingerprint (your own uptime checks, bulk sitemap pulls, unprotected catalogs), throughput matters more than pass rate, and a block costs you seconds, not accounts. Our Datacenter 50 at $20/month exists exactly for this.

Buy residential when…

The target grades traffic: SERPs, marketplaces, social, travel. Home IPs pass checks that stop datacenter ranges cold. Use rotation for discovery, sticky sessions for flows.

Buy static when…

Something must stay logged in. Rotation is the enemy of sessions — one dedicated IP beats a hundred rotating ones for accounts and carts.

Buy mobile when…

Nothing else passes. Carrier ASNs are the last word in trust, priced accordingly ($20–$50).

The rule of thumb: start bulk discovery on datacenter, move whatever converts to residential, pin whatever logs in to static.
