Getting Started

What is a proxy?

A proxy is a server that forwards your traffic, so websites see the proxy's IP instead of yours. Verdy sells private HTTP/HTTPS + SOCKS5 proxies: your own login, your own exit IPs, in the country you choose.

How long does activation take?

Verified payments (Paystack, Stripe, confirmed crypto) provision in seconds — credentials appear in your dashboard immediately. Manual-review payments activate on confirmation, typically within hours on business days.

What countries are available?

Kenya, Philippines, USA, Canada, UK, China (HK exit), Singapore, Japan, India, Germany, Netherlands. Live seats are shown in the portal; full countries pause new assignments.

Billing & Payments

How does billing work?

Checkout creates a numbered order first. Your payment satisfies the order; service activates only after the provider verifies it. Every charge produces a numbered invoice with PDF.

Which payment methods work?

Paystack (cards, bank, mobile money), Stripe cards, Crypto USDT and USDC. M-Pesa is coming soon.

How do renewals work?

Each verified payment adds 30/90/365 days stacked from the later of now or your current expiry — renewing early never wastes paid time. Expiry cuts access automatically (HTTP 407) until renewal.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, through the recorded refund process in our Refund Policy — provider refund attempted, subscription cancelled, everything logged.

Do coupons work?

Yes — percentage codes apply at order time with validity windows and usage caps enforced automatically.

Proxies

How do Residential proxies work?

Rotating residential gives a fresh home IP per request, or pin one with a sticky session: append _sess-<id> to your username. Sold by bandwidth quota (5/20/100 GB).

When should I choose Datacenter?

Bulk throughput where speed beats trust — sold as 10 or 50 IPs. For logins and high-trust targets, use residential or mobile instead.

What are Static proxies?

Dedicated proxies that keep the same IP (1, 5, or 20). Ideal for accounts, carts, and anything that punishes IP changes.

What are Mobile proxies?

Carrier-grade exits with the highest trust level, sold by bandwidth (5/20 GB). Best for social and app scraping.

How does bandwidth work?

Residential and Mobile products carry a GB quota shown in your dashboard with remaining balance. Static and Datacenter are per-proxy, unmetered.

Which protocols and ports?

HTTP/HTTPS and SOCKS5 on port 8899 in every country, authenticated with your username and password.

Security & Privacy

Do you log traffic?

No. We log account events (payments, tickets, admin actions) for billing and safety — never the content or destinations of your proxied traffic. See the Privacy Policy.

What happens on abuse reports?

Valid reports are triaged within 24h per our Abuse Handling Policy, up to suspension. Spam, fraud, and attacks are never permitted.

API & Support

How do I use the API?

Your proxy login is your API key: list endpoints, fetch credentials, check usage, rotate passwords. Snippets for cURL, Python, JavaScript, and Go are in the API portal.

Why do I get HTTP 407?

407 means unknown login, wrong password, or expired subscription. Check status in your dashboard; renewal restores access at the next 5-minute node sync.

Do you offer enterprise plans?

Volume and custom-country needs go through partnerships. Everyone else gets the same self-serve flow — support tickets with tracked status.
