How it works

1. Get your link

Every customer gets a unique referral code and link in the portal. Share it anywhere.

2. We track fairly

Clicks, signups inside the cookie window, and first paid subscriptions become conversions. Self-referrals are rejected; click farms are flagged.

3. Request payout

Above the minimum, request anytime. Payouts are recorded with method and status — no black box.
